Quantum computing capabilities aren’t a distant or hypothetical threat. Corporations and startups are making steady progress toward a functional quantum computer, and many experts predict true quantum computing is likely to arrive within the next decade. In preparation, governments have already released quantum-resistant cryptography standards with rapidly approaching deadlines.
