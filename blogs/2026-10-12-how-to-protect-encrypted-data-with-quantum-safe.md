Most quantum attack content focuses on protecting documents, files and digital signatures. But by intercepting authentication processes, future quantum systems and current attacks can threaten session data, credentials and network resources, too. This article defines quantum-safe authentication, how compromised authentication threatens organizations and why it matters now, even though quantum computers can’t break encryption yet.
