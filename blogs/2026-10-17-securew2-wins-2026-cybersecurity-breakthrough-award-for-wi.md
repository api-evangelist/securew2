A Wi-Fi password can give an attacker the same network access as the person it belongs to. Replacing shared credentials with certificate-based authentication changes how organizations verify who and what connects to their networks. That approach earned SecureW2 the title of “Wi-Fi Security Solution of the Year” in the 2026 CyberSecurity Breakthrough Awards.
